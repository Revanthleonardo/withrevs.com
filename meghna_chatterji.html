About Me

I am a conservation architect with a deep passion for heritage, community, and culture. With over a decade of experience, I have documented more than 2000 villages across India, worked on prestigious conservation projects, and taught as an award-winning academician. Today, through Kaarkhana and Zindagi Mubarak, I bridge architecture, music, and grassroots impact.
