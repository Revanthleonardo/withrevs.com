Pricing Structure

Affordable yet premium, reflecting spiritual & productive value in Pondicherry market.

  • Base Monthly Membership: Above ₹4,000 per person (includes 20 sqft dedicated space)
  • Per Sqft Rate (for extras): ₹200/sqft/month (e.g., additional private area or storage)
  • Flexible Options: Day Pass ₹500, Weekly ₹2,500, Monthly ₹4,500+
  • Upsells: Locker access ₹500/month, event hosting, lounge priority
